# HAWA launch post — ready to publish

This is a draft, not an already-published post or a submission URL.

Built HAWA for Challenge 6: Urban Air Quality Assistant in Google Prompt Wars by Hack2Skill.

HAWA helps families and communities navigate winter smog with personalized daily routines, indoor-air guidance and sustainable travel choices. It combines Open-Meteo modelled air data with household preferences, transparent impact calculations, Hindi guidance and a printable seven-day community kit.

OpenAI adds server-side explanations and grounded follow-up chat. Deterministic rules calculate numerical estimates, and clearly labelled fallbacks keep planning usable when external services fail. This is general guidance, not medical advice or a measurement of personal exposure.
