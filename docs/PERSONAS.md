# Household examples

- **Meera:** Delhi family, two children, higher-risk household, LPG kitchen, school-run car, low budget. Prioritise risk-aware guidance and feasible commute choices.
- **Ramesh:** older couple in Lucknow, indoor coal heating and morning walks. Hindi/simple wording, urgent coal/CO prevention before optional lifestyle measures.
- **Ananya:** Kolkata student, small leaky room, cycling, free budget. Avoid proposing expensive equipment as the only solution.
