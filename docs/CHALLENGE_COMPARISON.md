## What this review fixed

- Short questions such as `mask?`, `ｍａｓｋ？` and `मास्क?` now retrieve cited guidance. Punctuation and compatibility characters are normalised, repeated query words are deduplicated, and the knowledge search text is indexed once.
- Stopping chat cancels its stream reader. A late fetch or cancelled stream cannot replace a newer reply or enter conversation history. Unmount cancels outstanding work.
- Recognised urgent phrases are handled immediately on the device, including typographic apostrophes, without waiting for an AI request. This remains conservative phrase triage, not diagnosis.
- Output guards detect Unicode numbers and zero-width/full-width obfuscation. These are tested defensive layers, not proof against every prompt injection or unsafe sentence.
- Plans omit unavailable transit, work-from-home actions with zero allowed days, and carpool suggestions for non-car travel.
- Overall coverage gates require 90% lines, 85% statements, 80% branches and 80% functions; the domain retains 100% gates across all four metrics. CI's existing coverage command also executes the scenario evaluation and fails on any failed case.

## Reproduction and limits

Run `npm run test:coverage` and `npm run eval`. `reports/scenarios.json` records every case, individual checks, source revision and whether the working tree was dirty. It is an offline acceptance result, not an LLM judge score. Read [the quality report](QUALITY_REPORT.md) for final production checks and dated performance evidence.

The GitHub account billing lock still prevents hosted CI execution. Model budget and rate limits are process-local, not distributed account-wide controls. Human screen-reader testing, independent medical validation and real-model statistical evaluation remain outside the evidence collected here.
